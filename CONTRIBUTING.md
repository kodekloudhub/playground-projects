# Contributing

This repo is **generated** from the ClickUp "Projects List" doc by the `kk-catalog-sync`
skill. Do not hand-edit files under `projects/**` or the root `README.md` / `CATALOG.md` —
your changes will be overwritten on the next sync.

- **To change a project's content or code:** edit its page in ClickUp, then re-run the sync.
- **To change a project's URL slug or its lab-environment mapping:** edit `catalog.csv`
  (only the `slug` and `mapped_lab_env` columns are human-owned and preserved across syncs),
  then re-run the repo build.
